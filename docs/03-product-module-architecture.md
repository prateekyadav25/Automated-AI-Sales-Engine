- Leads, Accounts, Contacts, Pipeline, Tasks

## Full navigation (flagged / coming soon, never fake data)

- Market: intelligence, signals, ICPs, personas, target accounts, competitors (Phase 7 live: markets, signals, triggers)
- Acquisition: campaigns, advertising, ABM, content, landing pages, sources, attribution (Phase 8 live: inbound capture + dedupe; campaigns later)
- Sales: leads, contacts, accounts, pipeline, opportunities, tasks, sequences, forecast
- Engagement: email, voice, chat, meetings, conversation intelligence
- Commercial: products, pricing, business cases, quotes, proposals, approvals, contracts
- Customers: 360, onboarding, success plans, health, risks, QBR, usage
- Retention: renewals, churn, GRR, NRR
- Growth: upsell, cross-sell, whitespace, expansion, account plans
- Advocacy: references, case studies, testimonials, referrals, partners
- Intelligence, Automation, Knowledge, Administration

Feature flags hide unfinished engines. Coming-soon pages must not invent KPIs.
