# Sales Engine

Phases 2, 6, 10, 12, 13.

Default opportunity stages: Qualification, Discovery, Solution Fit, Technical Discovery, Demo, Business Case, Proposal, Commercial Discussion, Negotiation, Procurement, Legal, Commit, Closed Won, Closed Lost.

AI Deal Coach (Phase 13) **is live as rules-v1** and detects missing buyer, weak champion, stall, close-date slips, competitor risk, missing next step. Sequences (Phase 10) and meeting recaps (Phase 12) are desks in product.

Account 360 unifies company, contacts, committee, signals, pipeline, activities, contracts, success, renewals, expansion, and AI insights as those modules land.
