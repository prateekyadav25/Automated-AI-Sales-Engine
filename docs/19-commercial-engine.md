# Commercial Engine

Phase 14. **Implemented:** products, quotes, deterministic line/totals math. Discount ≥ 10% queues Approval Center. LLM does not invent totals.

CPQ: product, SKU, service, subscription, license, implementation, support, quantity, currency, discount, tax, duration, terms.

Discount approval example (configurable): under 10% sales manager, 10–20% director, 20–30% business head, over 30% CEO/finance.

Proposal workflow: Draft → Internal Review → Approved → Sent → Viewed → Revised → Negotiation → Accepted / Rejected.

Contract intelligence extracts dates, renewal, notice, pricing, SLAs. AI analysis is not legal advice.
