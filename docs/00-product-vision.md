# Product Vision

AGRAYIAN Autonomous Revenue OS is an AI-native revenue operating system for AGRAYIAN AI LABS.

It is not a CRM, not only lead management, not only sales automation, and not merely an AI SDR.

## Lifecycle

Intelligence → Acquire → Sell → Close → Succeed → Retain → Expand → Advocate → Learn

## Five continuous questions

1. WHERE should we sell?
2. WHO should we sell to?
3. WHAT should we sell?
4. HOW should we engage?
5. HOW do we retain and grow the customer?

## Human vs AI

AI executes routine work. AI recommends complex work. Humans approve important work and own strategic relationships.

Humans remain responsible for strategy, high-value relationships, sensitive communication, large commercial approvals, major negotiations, legal decisions, financial commitments, exceptions, governance, and escalations.

## Product test

Every module must contribute to one or more of: FIND, ACQUIRE, CONVERT, CLOSE, DELIVER, RETAIN, GROW, ADVOCATE, LEARN.

## Strategic metric

Maximum revenue productivity per human — not zero humans.
